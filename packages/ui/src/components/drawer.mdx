# Drawer

A panel that slides in from the edge of the screen. Drawers are commonly used
on mobile for navigation, filters, or secondary actions that don't require a
full context switch.

## Usage

- **Default** — a bottom sheet that slides up from the bottom of the screen with a header and footer.
- **Right Side** — the panel enters from the right edge, ideal for settings or detail views.
- **Open By Default** — the drawer is open on mount without requiring a trigger click.

## Do

- Use a bottom drawer on mobile screens where touch swipe-to-dismiss feels natural.
- Use a right-side drawer for persistent secondary panels like settings or filters.
- Always provide a clear close affordance (button or swipe handle).

## Don't

- Don't use a drawer for critical confirmations — use an AlertDialog instead.
- Don't nest drawers inside other overlays.
