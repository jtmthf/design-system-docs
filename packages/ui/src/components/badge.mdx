# Badge

A small, non-interactive label for status, categories, or counts. Badges
convey read-only metadata at a glance — they are not buttons and should
not trigger actions.

## Usage

- **Default** — primary emphasis; use for the most prominent status.
- **Secondary** — muted categorisation that sits alongside primary content.
- **Destructive** — errors, warnings, or critical status indicators.
- **Outline** — subtle, bordered style that works on any background.
- **Ghost** — minimal treatment for low-priority or decorative tags.
- **Link** — hyperlink-style badge for use inside rich text or metadata lines.

## Do

- Keep badge labels to one or two words.
- Use the `render` prop to render a badge as an `<a>` tag when the badge
  is genuinely navigable.
- Pair destructive badges with a tooltip or adjacent text for context.

## Don't

- Use a badge as a button — reach for `Button` or a clickable item instead.
- Use more than two or three badge variants on a single surface.
- Rely on colour alone to convey meaning; pair with a text label.
